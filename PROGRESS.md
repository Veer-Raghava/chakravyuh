### P2 · Dual graph — the actor floor, peel chains, mixer set-aside, `detail=actor` — DONE
  The brief: promote the actor/address graph to a **first-class scored surface beside the transaction
  graph** (the Elliptic++ shape), add a `serialize` `detail=actor` view, implement the **peel-chain
  tracer** and the **CoinJoin/mixer detector** (mark the tx and offer set-aside-with-reason so
  common-input-ownership is *not* applied across a mixer), keep `SAME_OWNER` a never-merging
  confidence-weighted edge with the 0.36 anchor, add node fields `peel_chain_id` and `coinjoin`, and
  keep the existing graph contract back-compatible. Every number below is measured on the canonical
  `agency-v2` run (32,787 transaction nodes, 121,294 addresses, 163,577 nodes / 1,206,827 edges).
  - **`jaal/trace.py` (new)** — the peel tracer. A hop is *peel-shaped* when the tx has exactly two
    outputs, unequal ones, and the smaller is ≤ 35% of `total_out` (`PEEL_MAX_SHARE_NUM/DEN = 7/20`,
    the arXiv 2205.13882 shape); a *chain* is two or more hops linked only through the change output
    the next hop spends. Stops are a closed vocabulary — `no_continuation`, `ambiguous_spend`,
    `chain_broken`, `cycle` — and `leak_frac` = peeled/initial per chain. Emits `graph/peel_chains.parquet`
    (`peel_chain_id`, `transactions`, `depth`, `initial_sats`, `peeled_sats`, `leak_frac`,
    `root_address`, `first_us`, `last_us`, `stop_reason`) plus `params.peel_tracer` in `_meta.json`
    with the rule, the share, `peel_shaped_transactions` / `chains` / `chained_transactions` /
    `isolated_hops`, so the manifest's accounting is one identity rather than four numbers.
  - **`jaal/mixer.py` (new)** — the collaborative-transaction detector: equal-value outputs **and**
    `n_out ≥ 3` **and** `n_in ≥ 2` (`MIXER_MIN_OUTPUTS`, `MIXER_MIN_INPUTS`). A marked tx gets
    `coinjoin = true` on its node, and both `common_input_edges` *and* `change_edges` gate on
    `~mixer.is_mixer()` — the set-aside the brief asked for, applied to **both** heuristics. The
    suppressed pairs are not dropped silently: `set_aside_edges()` counts them and `_meta.json`'s
    `params.mixer.suppressed_edges` reports `{"multi_input": 771, "change_addr": 0}` on agency-v2,
    under `set_aside_reason = "coinjoin_mixer"`.
  - **`jaal/build.py` / `jaal/cluster.py`** — nodes gain `peel_chain_id` and `coinjoin`;
    `_change_members` is factored out so `change_edges` and `set_aside_edges` read one derivation and
    cannot drift.
  - **`api/serialize.py` — the actor floor.** `graph(detail="actor")`: **address + cluster nodes,
    `SAME_OWNER` + `MEMBER_OF` edges, and scores** — no collapsed money edges. Scores per actor are
    degree, weakest/strongest `SAME_OWNER` confidence, `cluster_id`, and activity counted by
    **distinct transaction** (`n_transactions`, `n_coinjoin`, `n_peel_hops`, `peel_chains`) — an
    address paid twice by one collaborative tx counts once. `null` is kept distinct from `0`
    throughout (an address with no transactions in this capture has an unknown count, not a zero).
    The cap is split between the two kinds (clusters take at most half, by membership; addresses the
    rest, by degree) because a large run holds more clusters than the console has budget for nodes,
    and the response says so in numbers: `aggregated` carries `addresses`/`total_addresses` (1,000 of
    121,294) and `clusters`/`total_clusters` (1,000 of 6,532) plus the **omitted** node and edge kinds
    (`[asn, peer, transaction]`, `[ANNOUNCED_BY, HOSTED_IN, RECEIVES, SPENDS]`). `focus` **shows** the
    neighbourhood rather than intersecting it with the cap — `cluster:0` returns 12 nodes with its 11
    `MEMBER_OF` and 10 `SAME_OWNER` edges.
  - **`scripts/check_graph.py` — the from-outside checker, extended.** `_traced()` re-derives the peel
    shape *independently* of `trace.py` and asserts the marked↔chain round trip in both directions, the
    depth/`len(transactions)` identity, the stop vocabulary, `leak_frac == peeled/initial`, and the
    `first_us ≤ last_us` ordering; `_mixers()` asserts no tx is both a mixer and a peel hop and — tested
    from outside — that **no `multi_input` edge joins two of a mixer's own input addresses**.
  - **`docs/DATA-CONTRACTS.md` §5 (frozen)** — the two node rows and the `graph/peel_chains.parquet`
    column table, so the contract names what the stage writes.
