## R07 — JAAL measurement boundary and next-stage gate

The JAAL report now consolidates R02–R06. Its key limits are explicit: same-seed chronological splits are not entity-disjoint or generator-independent; score rows are output candidates, not wallet alerts; edge precision is not transitive cluster certainty; and no model has been promoted. “Source absent from captured data” is not inferable as a fact from no match. The future measurable quantity is candidate-set coverage/unknown-source probability under worlds with known inclusion and controlled capture censoring. Any such estimator must be calibrated at the campaign/entity/world level, include an `unknown / abstain` path, and test whether a true source address ever entered the observed universe. It cannot name the unseen person or turn a peer announcement into identity proof.

**Promotion gate:** do not feed a hard JAAL component label into BUDDHI as ground truth. Preserve observed address IDs, time-bounded evidence edges, confidence plus evidence provenance, and unresolved/collaborative flags. When proceeding to BUDDHI, evaluate with (a) answer-key entity labels only as held-out research targets, (b) JAAL-produced partitions as the operational input, and (c) a sensitivity grid across plausible merge precision/recall and capture coverage. This measures propagation of JAAL uncertainty rather than hiding it behind a single cluster assignment.
